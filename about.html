About Quran Kalima

Quran Kalima (کلمۂ قرآن) is a Quran vocabulary learning app designed to help you read the Quran with understanding. It combines word-by-word Quran reading with spaced-repetition flashcard learning, so you gradually build recognition of the words you encounter in the Book of Allah.

Key Features

  • All 114 Surahs with word-by-word translation in Urdu, English, and Hindi
  • Root and grammar analysis for every word
  • Spaced-repetition flashcards for active vocabulary recall
  • Progress tracking, streaks, and daily goals
  • Cloud sync across devices
