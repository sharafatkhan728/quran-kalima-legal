Quran Kalima — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026

Quran Kalima ("the App") is a Quran vocabulary learning app. This policy explains what information the App collects, how it is used, and the choices available to you.

Information We Collect

  • Account information: if you sign in, we store your name, email address, and profile photo (if provided) via Firebase Authentication.
  • Learning progress: which words you have marked known, your flashcard review history, reading position, bookmarks, and daily learning statistics — stored on your device and, if signed in, synced to our cloud database (Firebase Firestore).
  • Crash and diagnostic data: technical details sent to Firebase Crashlytics to help us fix bugs.
  • Usage analytics: anonymous, aggregated information about which screens/features are used, via Firebase Analytics.

Information We Do NOT Collect

  • We do not sell your personal data to third parties for advertising.
  • We do not access your contacts, photos, or files beyond what you explicitly upload as a profile picture.

How Your Information Is Used

Your learning data is used solely to provide the App's core features: tracking vocabulary progress, showing your reading position, scheduling review reminders, and syncing progress across devices you sign into.

Data Storage & Security

Most data is stored locally on your device. If you sign in, a copy is also stored in Firebase Firestore, protected by Google's standard security infrastructure and access rules restricting your data to your own account.

Notifications

The App may send local reminder notifications generated on your device from your own usage data — not from a remote server. Disable them anytime from your device's notification settings.

Third-Party Services

  • Firebase (Authentication, Firestore, Crashlytics, Analytics, App Check, Storage) — Google LLC
  • Google Sign-In — Google LLC

Your Choices

  • You can use most App features without signing in — your data then stays only on your device.
  • You can delete your account and cloud data anytime from the Profile screen, or via our Account Deletion page.

Children's Privacy

The App is suitable for general audiences including children, and does not knowingly collect personal information from children beyond what is described above for any user.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Continued use of the App after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
