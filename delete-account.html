How to Delete Your Quran Kalima Account

Option 1: Delete In-App (Recommended)

  1. Open the Quran Kalima app
  2. Go to the Profile tab
  3. Scroll to the Account section
  4. Tap Delete Account
  5. Confirm the deletion

What Gets Deleted

When you delete your account:

  • Your Firebase account (name, email, login credentials) is permanently removed
  • All cloud-synced data (known words, flashcard progress, bookmarks, reading position, streak) is permanently removed
  • All locally-stored progress on that device is also cleared

This action cannot be undone.

Option 2: Request Deletion by Email

If you are unable to access the app, email us at sharafatkhan728@gmail.com from the email address associated with your account, with the subject line "Account Deletion Request". We will process your request within 7 business days.
