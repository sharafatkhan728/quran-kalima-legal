How to Delete Your Quran Kalima Account

Option 1: Delete In-App (Recommended)

  1. Open the Quran Kalima app
  2. Go to the Profile tab
  3. Scroll to the Account section
  4. Tap Delete Account
  5. Confirm the deletion
  6. For security, sign in once more when asked (with your Google account or your password)

What Gets Deleted

When you delete your account:

  • Your account (name, email, login credentials) is permanently removed
  • All cloud-synced data (known words, flashcard progress, bookmarks, favourite surahs, reading position, streak, daily statistics) is permanently removed
  • Your profile settings and diagnostic data stored in the cloud are permanently removed
  • Your leaderboard profile, friend connections and friend requests are permanently removed
  • Your group memberships are removed. If you own a group that has other members, ownership passes to the longest-standing member; if you are the only member, the group is deleted
  • All locally-stored progress on that device is also cleared

This action cannot be undone.

What May Remain For a Short Time

Crash reports that were tagged with your account ID are kept by Firebase Crashlytics for a limited period (generally about 90 days) under Google's own retention rules, and are not deleted immediately. Anonymous, aggregated analytics are not linked to your identity.

Option 2: Request Deletion by Email

If you are unable to access the app, email us at sharafatkhan728@gmail.com from the email address associated with your account, with the subject line "Account Deletion Request". We will process your request within 7 business days.
